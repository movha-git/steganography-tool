⚙️ How LSB Steganography Works

Every pixel has 3 colour values: Red, Green, Blue (each 0–255).

In binary, 255 = 11111111. The last bit (LSB) barely changes the colour at all.

We replace that last bit with bits from your secret message. The image looks completely identical to the human eye — but your message is hidden inside every pixel.
